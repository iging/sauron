# Organic Biophilic

Leaf green, clay neutrals, soft irregular shapes, breathing room.

- **Fits:** Wellness, gardens, sustainability, natural food, outdoor brands.
- **Avoid for:** Hard-tech products, finance.

## Depth

Surface tint and natural, low, wide shadows.

## Signature Moves

- Leaf or pebble shapes as image masks.
- Earth-toned alternating bands.
- Hand-drawn line icons.

## Never

- Neon accents.
- Hard grid lines.
- Environmental claims without evidence.
