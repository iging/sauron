# Luxury Noir

Warm black, champagne metal, slow confident spacing.

- **Fits:** Jewellery, hotels, fragrance, private services, high-end property.
- **Avoid for:** Budget offers, utility tools, busy catalogues.

## Depth

Hairlines in the metal tone. Photography supplies the depth.

## Signature Moves

- Uppercase micro labels with wide tracking.
- Full-bleed photography with small captions.
- One metal-toned rule beneath headings.

## Never

- Fast or bouncy motion.
- Bright saturated colour.
- Crowded viewports.
