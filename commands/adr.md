# /adr Command

Documents significant architectural decisions and technical trade-offs.

---

## 1. Identity & Execution

- **Lead Agent:** Aragorn
- **Specialist Agent:** architect
- **Category:** Architecture

---

## 2. Trigger Syntax

```bash
/adr <decision-title>
```

---

## 3. Workflow Protocol

1. **Step 1:** Document context, options considered, and chosen solution.
2. **Step 2:** List positive and negative consequences.
3. **Step 3:** Record decision status in docs/adr/.

---

## 4. Hard Verification Gates

- Must state at least two alternative options evaluated.
